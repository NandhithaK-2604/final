Important currency-conversion rule:
The amount in words must correspond to the original account balance and its original currency.
Do NOT generate the words from the converted amount.
Example: if the account balance is €2,000,000 and the selected display currency is USD showing ≈ $1,818,181.82, the words must still be “Two Million Euros Only.”
The converted amount should remain separate and unchanged.
If displaying the total available funds in words, generate the words based on the total shown in the currently selected display currency.
Design requirements:
Do NOT display the text “Amount in words”.
Do NOT add a “View in words” button.
Do NOT use a popup or modal.
Do NOT redirect to another page.
Keep the written amount subtle and secondary.
Use a smaller font than the numeric balance.
Use muted text color.
Allow the written amount to wrap naturally to a maximum of 2 lines.
Keep all account cards equal in height.
Use consistent spacing and alignment.
Add a very subtle divider between the numeric/converted amount and the written amount if necessary.
Do not make the written amount bold.
Do not make the cards excessively tall.
Maintain a clean, professional enterprise-banking appearance.
Make the amount in words dynamically generated from the actual numeric balance and currency; do not hardcode values.
