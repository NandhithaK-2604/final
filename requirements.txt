1. Overall layout

The current page feels too large and has unnecessary empty space.

Make the page more compact while keeping it comfortable to read.

- Reduce the width/height of unnecessary containers.
- Reduce excessive padding and margins.
- Reduce the vertical gap between the batch information and Payee Details.
- Keep the main content centered and properly aligned.
- Do not stretch the content to fill the entire screen.
- Avoid one huge oversized card with large empty areas.

The page should have a similar density and spacing rhythm to the existing Approve page.
